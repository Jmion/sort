I am writing to express my strong interest in the Software Engineer position for the Observe platform at Snowflake in Zurich. My background is centered around building and owning distributed data systems. One of my key challenges was designing an optimized schema and ingestion pipeline for a large scale database that integrated messy data from multiple open source repositories. I solved this by implementing a hybrid architecture of raw ingestion tables and pre-aggregated query tables, which minimized joins and kept query performance highly predictable under heavy load. This directly aligns with Observe's objective of processing hundreds of terabytes of telemetry data efficiently and at a low cost.

That experience taught me that observability systems are only as good as the developer experience they provide. If an alert does not point directly to the root cause, it is just noise.

The opportunity to work on Observe by Snowflake excites me because of the engineering approach you are taking. Building a platform that consolidates metrics, logs, and traces using open standards like Apache Iceberg on top of the Snowflake AI Data Cloud is a logical step forward for enterprise infrastructure. I enjoy working on foundational systems where system design, reliability, and low latency are core requirements. I thrive in teams that prioritize clean, readable code and practical infrastructure design over unnecessary complexity.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my systems engineering background and data processing experience can contribute to the team in Zurich. Thank you for your time and consideration.
